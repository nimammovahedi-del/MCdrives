How it's built

From open map data to a city you can drive.

Map Craft is built by a pipeline that runs from start to finish with one command. Here's what it does, what it uses, and how a 1.2 GB city fits in a phone's browser.

The pipeline

Six steps, one command.

The same build runs on GitHub's servers and publishes the finished city automatically.

STEP 01

Gather open data

Roads, lanes, buildings, parks and water from OpenStreetMap; places and addresses from Overture; ground height from USGS LiDAR.

OSMOvertureUSGS 3DEP
STEP 02

Bake the blocks

The open-source Arnis generator turns the data into a block world, about one meter per block, in 41 chunks built side by side.

ArnisGitHub Actions
STEP 03

Pack for streaming

Only visible block faces are kept, merged into large flat pieces and packed into 512 × 512 block squares with height tiles.

Greedy meshingBinary packs
STEP 04

Build the road graph

Every street becomes a routing graph with one-ways, ramps, gated streets and an arrival point for each place.

A* routingArrival points
STEP 05

Drive in the browser

A three.js viewer streams the squares around the car and draws the route, camera, sky, cars and 2D map.

three.jsWebGL
STEP 06

Add live traffic

A small server gathers Caltrans, CHP and TomTom feeds every few minutes, and the app routes around trouble.

Cloudflare Workers

The city in 41 chunks

Built in parallel, joined at the seams.

Irvine is cut into a grid of 5.4 km² chunks. Only the 41 that touch the city are built, at the same time, then stitched back together so roads and ground line up across every seam (6,652 road nodes joined). Brighter chunks hold more detail.

Why it runs on a phone

A 1.2 GB city, a few MB at a time.

The whole city would never fit in a phone's browser. So it's split into 1,025 squares, and Map Craft only loads the ones around your car, dropping them again as you drive away.

The whole city1.19 GB
One square (512 × 512 blocks)1.2 MB
Loaded around your car, about15 MB

About 13 squares sit within 750 blocks of the car at any time. Average square size from the current Irvine build.
