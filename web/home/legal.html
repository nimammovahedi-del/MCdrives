Legal & Privacy

The short version: Map Craft is a free beta, it has no accounts, and your location never leaves your device.

Not for use while driving

Map Craft is a beta built for fun and exploration. Don't use it to navigate a real vehicle, and never interact with a screen while driving. Map data can be out of date or wrong.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • If you allow location, your position is used in your browser to show the blue dot and start directions. It is not sent to us.
  • Your settings, like your chosen car and map style, are saved in your own browser's local storage.
  • To show live traffic, the app downloads traffic data for Orange County from our traffic server. These requests don't include your location.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
