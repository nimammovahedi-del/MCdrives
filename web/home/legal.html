Not for use while driving

Map Craft is a free beta, made for fun and exploration. Don't use it to navigate a real vehicle, and never look at or touch a screen while you drive. Map and traffic data can be late, missing or wrong.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • If you allow location, your position is used inside your browser to show the blue dot and start directions. It is never sent to us.
  • Your settings, like your car and map style, are saved in your own browser's storage and stay on your device.
  • For live traffic, the app downloads Orange County traffic data from our traffic server. Those requests don't include your location.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
