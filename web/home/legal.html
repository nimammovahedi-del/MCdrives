The details

In plain words.

The rules for using Map Craft, written to be read.

Not for use while driving

Map Craft is a free beta, made for fun. Don't use it to navigate a real vehicle, and never look at or touch a screen while you drive. Map and traffic data can be late, missing or wrong.

Privacy
  • No accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • If you allow location, it is used inside your browser for the blue dot and directions. It is never sent to us.
  • Settings such as your car and map style are saved in your browser's storage, on your device.
  • Live traffic is downloaded from our traffic server. Those requests don't include your location.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
